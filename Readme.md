Project Title
    AI-Based Smart Governance & Compliance Monitoring System for Coal Mines

1. Problem
Coal mines have to continuously monitor safety, inspections, statutory compliance, contractor activities and corrective actions. When information is recorded or maintained across different channels, it can become difficult for management to get a unified and timely view of compliance status.
    
2. Objective
To develop a centralized system that can help mine management collect, organize and monitor safety and compliance information, identify important gaps, and support faster follow-up and decision-making.

3. Target Users
    -Mine managers
    Safety officers
    Inspection teams
    Surveyors and field staff
    Contractor supervisors
    Corporate/subsidiary compliance teams
    Relevant regulatory authorities
    
4. Existing Workflow
Field inspection → Observation recorded → Data consolidated → Management review → Corrective action → Follow-up
The key issue is that information may be distributed across different records and channels, making centralized visibility and timely follow-up difficult.
    
5. Proposed Solution
A centralized AI-enabled governance and compliance platform that can:
Digitally record inspection observations
Maintain safety and compliance records
Track corrective actions
Monitor contractor compliance
Provide a centralized dashboard
Generate alerts for pending/overdue actions
Support geo-tagged and time-stamped field records
Provide analytics for identifying recurring compliance issues

6. AI/Smart Features
The AI component can be used for:
Risk-pattern identification
Detecting recurring safety/compliance issues
Prioritizing high-risk observations
Generating compliance insights
Predicting areas requiring attention based on historical records
The exact AI model should be finalized after understanding the actual data available from mine operations.

7. Innovation
The innovation is not simply digitizing inspection forms. The proposed system combines field-level reporting + compliance tracking + corrective-action monitoring + centralized governance + AI-based risk insights in one platform.

8. Expected Benefits
Better visibility of mine-wide compliance status
Easier tracking of corrective actions
Reduced dependence on scattered records
Faster escalation of important issues
Better documentation and traceability
Data-driven safety and governance decisions

9. Scope
Included:
Safety inspections
Compliance monitoring
Contractor compliance tracking
Corrective-action tracking
Centralized reporting
Risk analytics
Not included initially:
Payroll/HR management
Complete statutory/legal decision-making
Replacement of regulatory authorities' functions

10. Current Validation Status
At present, the project is supported by secondary/public evidence and the existing problem context. Direct mine-site interviews and site visits have not yet been completed, so numerical claims about user satisfaction, reporting delays or adoption should be treated as validation targets rather than established results.

11. Phase 2 Plan
Validate the problem with mine-industry stakeholders.
Study the actual inspection and compliance workflow.
Identify the most important gaps and user requirements.
Develop 3 different solution concepts.
Test the concepts with potential users.
Select the concept supported by the strongest evidence.
Build and test a prototype.
One-line Project Summary
“A centralized AI-enabled platform to improve safety, compliance visibility, corrective-action tracking and governance across coal-mine operations.”
